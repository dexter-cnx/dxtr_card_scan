## Gate decision

The 1.0 roadmap item `validated Android/iOS/macOS support` may be checked only when:

1. all general Android rows above are `pass`;
2. all general iOS rows above are `pass`;
3. all macOS rows above are `pass`;
4. failures have either been fixed and re-tested or explicitly removed from the claimed supported behavior.

The live-geometry sections do **not** block general manual capture support, but they continue to block production auto-capture calibration/threshold promotion until their evidence is complete.

## Gate decision

The 1.0 roadmap item `validated Android/iOS/macOS support` may be checked only when **all** of the following are true:

1. the Candidate section has a concrete package commit/tag, validation date, and validator; none may remain `TBD`;
2. Android records a concrete device/model, Android version, camera plugin version, and evidence path/ZIP; none may remain `TBD`;
3. iOS records a concrete device/model, iOS version, camera plugin version, and evidence path/ZIP; none may remain `TBD`;
4. macOS records a concrete Mac model, macOS version, and evidence path; none may remain `TBD`;
5. all general Android rows above are `pass`;
6. all general iOS rows above are `pass`;
7. all macOS Gallery/image-processing rows above are `pass`;
8. failures have either been fixed and re-tested or explicitly removed from the claimed supported behavior.

A table full of `pass` results is not sufficient by itself: the provenance fields above are mandatory so the validation is attributable to a specific release candidate, validator, hardware/host, OS, and evidence artifact.

The live-geometry sections do **not** block general manual capture support, but they continue to block production auto-capture calibration/threshold promotion until their evidence is complete.
